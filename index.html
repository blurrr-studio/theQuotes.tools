Quotes is the solution for

  • Broken focus. No more interrupting your reading flow to scribble notes or switch apps. Capture seamlessly, stay immersed.
  • Scattered knowledge. Consolidate your highlights from books, articles, and even photos of text into one single, searchable library.

Mission of Quotes

Lower the Friction. Amplify the Value.

Capturing and organizing reading notes often demands more effort than it gives back, turning a meaningful practice into a chore. We believe that’s a problem worth solving.

With Quotes, we work to lighten the effort of collecting and organizing, so your focus stays on reading, thinking, and creating.

And beyond storage, Quotes brings your saved words back into daily life—resurfacing them at the right moments, turning highlights into living tools that shape thought and spark inspiration.
